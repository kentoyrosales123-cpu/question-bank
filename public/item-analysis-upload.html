Required Format

Student Name | Student ID | Section | Item 1 | Item 2 | ... | Total Score

  • Use 1 or C for correct.
  • Use 0 or W for wrong.
  • For problem-solving, enter the earned numeric score per item.
  • Rubric sheets with headers like Item 1 | CO1 | SO | PI | Bloom | Max | Target are accepted.
  • Blank item values are accepted and treated as wrong.
  • Total Score may be blank; the system can compute it.
